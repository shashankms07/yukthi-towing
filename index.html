COMMON QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you provide towing 24 hours a day?

Yes. We are available 24/7, including nights, weekends and holidays.

Which areas of Bangalore do you cover?

We cover Bangalore and nearby areas. Call us with your location and we will confirm availability.

Do you tow bikes as well as cars?

Yes. We handle cars, bikes and scooters, as well as accident recovery and long-distance towing.

How is towing priced?

Pricing depends on the vehicle type, distance and situation. We share the charge on the phone before dispatching.

What should I do while waiting for the tow truck?

Move to a safe spot if you can, switch on hazard lights, keep your documents and keys ready, and share your live location with us on WhatsApp.
